About

Engineering that earns trust.

For nine years I've helped companies turn ambitious ideas into dependable software. My home turf is the Microsoft stack — C#, .NET Core and .NET Framework, ASP.NET MVC and Web API — paired with modern front-end work in React, Angular and Vue. I design the data layer as carefully as the interface, and I care as much about how a team ships as what it ships.

Across ERP, retail and enterprise product teams I've owned features end to end — gathering requirements, architecting the solution, writing the code, hardening it with tests, and shipping it through CI/CD pipelines. I've mentored developers, partnered with QA and leadership, and consistently delivered high-quality results under real-world deadlines.

I'm as comfortable with clients as I am in the codebase. On an on-site engagement in Oslo, Norway, I worked face-to-face with the client — understanding their needs, shaping them into clear requirements, and building the software to deliver on them.
